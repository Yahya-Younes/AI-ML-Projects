Rules that make the plan work

  • Same time every study day, phone in another room. Short evening blocks (30 min) are for the trainer or a short lab.
  • Every "Learn" or "Watch" block ends with a few trainer questions on that topic.
  • Log every wrong answer the same day, as one line: constraint word in the scenario → right service or setting. That log becomes your second cheatsheet.
  • Never skip two study days in a row. A missed day moves to your next long (weekend) day, not on top of tomorrow.
  • Agent labs need a chat model deployment with quota. If your subscription shows 0 quota, request an increase on day one: it is free but can take a few days. Until then, read the lab steps and answer the matching trainer questions.
  • From the exam-mode phase on, no new material: only the trainer, your mistakes log and the cheatsheet.
  • You are ready when the official practice assessment is at 80%+ and a trainer simulation is at 800+, not when you feel ready. If you are below 700 two days before, move the exam (free up to 24 h before).
